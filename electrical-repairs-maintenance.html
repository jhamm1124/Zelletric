Reliable Repairs You Can Count On

Electrical problems rarely happen at a convenient time. Whether it's a breaker that keeps tripping, an outlet that stopped working, or lights that flicker for no obvious reason, Zellectric diagnoses the issue quickly and fixes it right the first time. We carry the tools and experience to handle everything from a single faulty switch to a full electrical system tune-up, and every repair includes a clear explanation of what went wrong and how we fixed it.

Beyond one-off repairs, we also offer preventive maintenance to catch small issues before they become expensive problems — giving you peace of mind that your home or business's electrical system is safe and up to code.

Signs You May Need an Electrician

  • Flickering or dimming lights
  • Breakers that trip repeatedly
  • Warm, discolored, or buzzing outlets
  • Outlets or switches that stopped working
  • A burning smell near outlets or the panel
  • Frequent power surges or fluctuations
